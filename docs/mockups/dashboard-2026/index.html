Dashboard — 2026 changes

Three ways to keep the period picker on screen while you scroll. Every page carries the same sample figures and links the app's real stylesheet, so what you see is what ships. Open one, scroll, and the switcher at the foot of the page moves you between them. None of this touches your database — the numbers are made up.

Pick one

Option A
Sticky header
The whole header pins: title on the left, controls on the right, exactly where they already are. The title shrinks a little once you scroll and a hairline appears underneath.
Costs ~62px of height, always · Nothing new to learn
Option B
Compact bar
The big title scrolls away and a slim bar takes over, holding just the controls. A small "Dashboard" label fades in on the left once the real title has gone.
Costs ~44px of height · Two titles to keep in step
Option C
Floating pill
The header scrolls away for good. Past 110px the controls re-form as a rounded pill floating over the content. They are moved, not copied, so there is only ever one set.
Costs nothing permanent · Something moves as you scroll

The same in all three

  • Cash Flow Calendar is gone. The Spending Heatmap stays — it covers the same ground over a whole year.
  • Income by Category is new. Same bars, same colours, same drill-down as Expenses by Category, sitting directly beneath it.
  • Monthly Summary rows open the month. Click any row for every transaction in it. A chevron shows on hover; the note button still works on its own.
