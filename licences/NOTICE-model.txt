The weights are the Q4_K_M GGUF conversion published at
https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.5-4B-GGUF, downloaded on first use rather
than carried in the download, because 2.7 GB does not belong in an application
bundle.

Apache 2.0 permits redistribution and asks two things in return: that its terms
travel with the work, and that the attribution above is preserved. Both files
are copied next to the model when it is fetched. That repository declares the
licence in its metadata but ships no licence file, so this copy is the model's
own, taken from the weights as published.

The inference server is llama.cpp (https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp),
MIT licensed; its notice travels in vendor/llama/ with the binaries.
